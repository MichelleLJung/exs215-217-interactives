Planning principles

Choose techniques appropriate for the participant and available equipment. Allow time for explaining the purpose, demonstrating pressure control, teaching modifications, moving between positions, and closing the session. Clean and return equipment after the session has ended.

Reference: FiTOUR Myofascial Release with Foam Roller, Chapter 10 (foam rolling basics) and Chapters 11–14 (roller-specific techniques). The planner references the certification rather than reproducing its illustrations or instructions.
